# Privacy Policy — Rope Untangle

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy covers the mobile game **Rope Untangle** (Android package `com.mrrak.ropes`), referred to below as "the game".

## Summary

The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect or receive your personal information. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. Those two services process some data, described below.

## Information the game stores on your device

To keep your place in the game, the following is saved locally on your device only:

- your progress (levels solved, stars, best results, unlocked boards),
- your coin balance and the items you own,
- your settings (language, music and sound on or off, chosen pins, board and background).

This information is not sent to the developer. It is removed when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game.

## Advertising

The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one in exchange for coins. Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device,
- your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) may be derived,
- device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size),
- information about how you interact with the ads and basic diagnostics.

Depending on your region and your choices, this data may be used to show personalised ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

How Google uses this data is described at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Purchases

Coin packs can be bought with real money. Payments are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. The developer never sees your card number or other payment details; the game receives only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, so that the coins can be added. Google's handling of payment data is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Information the game does not collect

The game does not ask for or access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files.

## Permissions

The game uses Internet access to load ads and to complete purchases, and the advertising ID permission required by Google AdMob. The puzzle itself can be played offline.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please get in touch so it can be addressed.

## Data retention and your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to export or delete. Game progress is on your device and is removed by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. For data held by Google (ads and purchases), you can use the controls in your Google Account and the links above. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data; these requests are directed to Google for the data it holds.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the game changes how data is handled. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
